Access pathway
Train. Certify.
Build independently.
A structured ladder gates machine access behind training — ensuring safety at every level.
L1

General Induction

Space orientation, safety rules, emergency procedures, and code of conduct. Required for all users before accessing any machine.

90 minAll usersNo prerequisitesSat 10AM
L2

Category Training

Machine-category specific training: 3D printing, laser cutting, woodworking, electronics. Theory + quiz + supervised demo run.

2 hrsL1 requiredQuiz included
L3

Machine Certification

Model-specific SOP, safety checklist sign-off, supervised job run, and certification quiz. Unlocks independent booking of that specific machine.

L2 requiredSOP reviewSupervised run
L4

Independent Use

Unlocked after 2+ successful supervised sessions. Self-service booking, extended slot eligibility, and priority queue access.

Auto-unlock2 supervised runsSolo booking
L5

Advanced / Staff-Approved

High-risk machines, metalworking, after-hours access. Staff-approved on a case-by-case basis. Annual refresher required.

Staff approvalAnnual renewalAfter-hours
