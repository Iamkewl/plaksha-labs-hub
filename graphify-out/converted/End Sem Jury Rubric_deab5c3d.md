# ILGC-2 End Sem Jury Rubric (30% weightage)
The students will be evaluated through their team’s presentation. The time slot for each group will be 20 minutes where; 10 mins (for the presentation) +10 mins (for Q&A). The following will be the evaluation component, along with their description.

| Component | What we are looking for | Marks (30) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identified Problem | Is the problem identified clear, interesting, and relevant in the context of Plaksha Campus? 

Here we want to gauge their empathy and observational ability. | 3 |
| Background Research | What is already available, and what are the gaps in the context of their problem & potential solution or the desired level of service/product which is the crux of the problem? | 2 |
| Stakeholders’ Context 
& Connected Data | Is the problem identified and solution proposed relevant in context of its stakeholders 
Is the claim supported with relevant quantitative/qualitative data
Are the collected and presented data relevant, reliable, trustworthy, suitable and sufficient?
Is the primary and secondary data collected/presented by the students relevant?

They are supposed to collect data at two stages: a) at the time of problem identification and b) after they converge on the best solution (stakeholders should show their agreement and/or satisfaction with the solution). | 3+3+2+2 |
| Solution | Is the proposed solution clear, relevant, and workable?

Here we want to gauge their ability of project planning, team’s skill mapping and understanding of resource planning (costing, negotiation skill, understanding of RoI, etc.).

They have to declare which AI tool they have used to arrive upon the solution, what prompt they gave to the tool and how did they finally conclude with the tool. After that what value addition the team did to finalize the solution. | 5 |
| Proposed Proto Design | Are high-level description of how the prototype works and its key features, given?
What was the group’s rationale behind choosing the proposed solution over the others (supported with relevant data/outcomes)

They are not supposed to bring any working solution. But they have to establish/defend why their proposed solution would work. How did they refine/improve the solution, on what basis? Which article/blog/ discussion forum helped them think so? Is testing part of their solution’s success/efficiency?

Here also they have to declare which AI tool they have used to arrive upon the solution, what prompt they gave to the tool and how did they finally conclude with the tool. After that what value addition the team did to finalize the solution. | 10 |
